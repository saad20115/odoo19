Employee Attendance WebCam module

Take images with a WebCam when employees check-in and check-out from attendance.

Webcam

  • Adds a webcam element on the check-in / check-out form for the employees to automatically take a photo when clicked.
  • The browser asks for permission to the webcam the first time when you hover over the webcam element.
